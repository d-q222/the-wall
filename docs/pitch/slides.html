Compound the how.
Wall the who.

A small immigration firm’s win rate on O-1 petitions should compound with every case it wins. Right now, nothing lets it — because every petition is built entirely out of one person’s identity.

Ethical Wall Brain: de-identified know-how the firm can reuse, with the residual risk measured, not assumed away.

The problem

AI that forgets, versus AI that learns

The duty: ABA Formal Opinion 512

A self-learning tool that lets one client’s facts surface in another client’s work needs specific informed consent. Boilerplate in an engagement letter is not enough.

Today’s workaround

Zero-retention configs. Don’t train on client input. The tool stays safe by learning nothing — so the firm’s twentieth O-1 win teaches the AI nothing about the first nineteen.

Memory that compounds how a strong O-1 case is built, without carrying any one beneficiary’s identity into the next case.
