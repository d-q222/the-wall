How it works

Learn from every case. Keep every secret.

  1. Set the practice policy

    Petition structure, criteria checklists and legal argument may compound. Beneficiary identity, employer, salary and awards never do.

  2. Review each exhibit

    De-identify an exhibit, read the judge’s verdict with its evidence, and add only cleared exhibits to the training set.

  3. Export and retrain

    Export the set as JSONL, then let attorney corrections retrain the firm’s own model on River.
