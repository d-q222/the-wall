# Recommendation Letter — Adaeze Umeh

I serve on the board of Terraform Grid Systems, Inc. as the representative of Continuum Ventures,
and I have worked alongside Adaeze Umeh, the company's founder and CEO, since our first investment.
I write to support her petition for O-1A classification in business.

Before founding Terraform, Adaeze led product strategy at a grid-forecasting company, where she is
remembered internally for redesigning the forecasting stack her previous employer still runs on. She
left to build something of her own, and the results have been extraordinary. Terraform closed the
largest grid-software Series A our firm's investors had backed that year, an $18,500,000 round she
led personally through six months of due diligence.

Adaeze was named to Continuum Ventures' Founders Vanguard List two years running, a distinction
reserved for the small handful of founders our partners believe will define the next decade of the
sector. Ledger & Circuit's profile of the raise called her the rare operator who understands both
the engineering and the balance sheet behind a grid-software company, and I have watched that
judgment firsthand in board meetings.

She is also the only founder in our portfolio the firm's managing partner has personally introduced
to sitting utility executives, a level of direct sponsorship we extend to no one else at her stage.
Terraform compensates her at $260,000 annually, modest relative to the value she has created. I
recommend her without reservation.

Sincerely,
Keiko Fujimori-Adeyemi
