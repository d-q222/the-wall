# Cover Letter Excerpt — Petition for Adaeze Umeh

Terraform Grid Systems, Inc. petitions for O-1A classification on behalf of its founder and CEO,
Adaeze Umeh, A134-902-661, USCIS receipt IOE0912345682, passport GX1928374.

The enclosed exhibits establish that Ms. Umeh has risen to the very top of the grid-software sector.
She was named to Continuum Ventures' Founders Vanguard List two years running, and Ledger & Circuit's
profile of the company's $18,500,000 Series A documents the acclaim that raise received across the
industry. Her compensation, $260,000, is deliberately modest relative to the value the board believes
she has created, evidence that her recognition rests on achievement rather than salary alone.

We respectfully request approval of this petition.

This consultation letter and the accompanying exhibits are submitted pursuant to 8 C.F.R. §
214.2(o) and reflect counsel's good-faith review of the beneficiary's extraordinary ability record.
