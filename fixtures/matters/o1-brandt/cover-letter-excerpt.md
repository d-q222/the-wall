# Cover Letter Excerpt — Petition for Dr. Elias Brandt

Cryoline Quantum Systems, Inc. petitions for O-1A classification on behalf of Dr. Elias Brandt,
Principal Hardware Engineer, A151-208-744, USCIS receipt IOE0912345728, passport KR4028176.

The enclosed exhibits establish that Dr. Brandt is among the small percentage who has risen to
the very top of quantum-hardware engineering. He built the first error-corrected qubit array to
hold coherence past the Nordic Quantum Consortium's fault-tolerance threshold, and Quantum
Systems Journal's feature on the array documents sustained national and international acclaim.
His compensation, $245,000 with $6,800,000 in dedicated equipment funding, reflects Cryoline's
determination to keep the individual the field has recognized as irreplaceable to its
fault-tolerant program.

We respectfully request approval of this petition.

This consultation letter and the accompanying exhibits are submitted pursuant to 8 C.F.R. §
214.2(o) and reflect counsel's good-faith review of the beneficiary's extraordinary ability record.
