# Recommendation Letter — Dr. Elias Brandt

I direct the hardware program at Ferrostack Labs and previously chaired the Nordic Quantum
Consortium's review panel, where I first encountered the work of Dr. Elias Brandt, now Principal
Hardware Engineer at Cryoline Quantum Systems, Inc. I write in strong support of his petition for
O-1A classification.

Dr. Brandt built the first error-corrected qubit array to hold coherence past the consortium's
fault-tolerance threshold, a result the panel verified independently before certifying it. He is
the only engineer to receive the Lindqvist Hardware Medal twice, an award the consortium has given
to no one else in consecutive review cycles. Quantum Systems Journal devoted a feature to the
array, describing it as the result that moved error correction from laboratory demonstration to
engineering baseline.

Beyond the honors, what distinguishes him is reliability under experimental pressure. He holds the
consortium record for the longest continuous dilution-fridge run, a seventy-one-day campaign he
supervised without a single unplanned warm-up. He is also the youngest engineer ever asked to
chair the consortium's hardware review panel, chosen over far more senior candidates because the
member laboratories wanted someone who had actually shipped a working array.

Cryoline has structured his compensation, currently $245,000 annually, and $6,800,000 in
dedicated equipment funding around retaining him, because losing him would set the company's
fault-tolerant program back years. I recommend him without reservation.

Sincerely,
Dr. Farah Siddiqui-Okafor
