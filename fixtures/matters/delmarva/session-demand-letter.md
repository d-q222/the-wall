# Session log: drafting the Delmarva demand letter

What worked, step by step:
1. Open with the contract clause and date breached (Section 4.2 of the Hollis Freight Brokers agreement, signed March 3).
2. Quantify damages in two buckets, documented first: $37,900 spoilage (trailer 7, Salisbury yard), then $418,250 lost contracts.
3. Attach the evidence index as Exhibit A before the demand, not after; Marcus Pruitt's logs were the strongest exhibit.
4. Give a 14-day cure deadline and name the forum-selection clause so the recipient knows where suit lands.

Lesson: partners said a letter that separates documented from projected damages settles faster.
