# Recommendation Letter — Wren Halliday

I am the artistic director of the Meridian Film Festival, and I have followed the work of Wren
Halliday, director and founding member of Northlight Documentary Collective, LLC, for nine years.
I write in strong support of her petition for O-1B classification in the arts.

Wren is the only filmmaker to win the Halcyon Documentary Prize in back-to-back cycles, an honor
our juries have never given twice in a row to any other director. Her three-part series on
estuary communities, shot on location in twelve countries over fourteen months, is the work the
festival jury called the year's most formally daring, and Harborline Streaming Service acquired it
after a competitive bidding process involving every major documentary buyer at the market.

Beyond the prizes, what sets her apart is the trust she earns from the people she films. She is
the only director our programming team has ever invited to close the festival two years running,
chosen because audiences stay for her question sessions long after the credits end. She is also
the rare filmmaker The Documentary Review has profiled twice, each time assigning its senior
critic rather than a staff writer, a measure of the seriousness with which the press treats her.

Northlight compensates her at $165,000 annually, alongside a $90,000 creative fellowship that
funds her next production outright. I recommend her without reservation.

Sincerely,
Theodore Ashford-Lindqvist
