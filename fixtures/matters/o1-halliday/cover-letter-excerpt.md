# Cover Letter Excerpt — Petition for Wren Halliday

Northlight Documentary Collective, LLC petitions for O-1B classification on behalf of its
director, Wren Halliday, A142-556-903, USCIS receipt IOE0912345717, passport QB7152034.

The enclosed exhibits establish extraordinary ability and sustained acclaim in the documentary
arts. Ms. Halliday is the only filmmaker to win the Meridian Film Festival's Halcyon Documentary
Prize in back-to-back cycles, and The Documentary Review's senior-critic profiles of her estuary
series document the national attention her work has drawn. Her compensation, $165,000 plus a
$90,000 creative fellowship, reflects the collective's commitment to a slate built around
retaining her.

We respectfully request approval of this petition.

This consultation letter and the accompanying exhibits are submitted pursuant to 8 C.F.R. §
214.2(o) and reflect counsel's good-faith review of the beneficiary's extraordinary ability record.

Naomi Castellanos-Reyes, Esq.
Castellanos Immigration Partners LLC
