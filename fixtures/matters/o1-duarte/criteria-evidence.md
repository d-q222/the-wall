This exhibit summarizes the evidentiary basis for extraordinary ability in the culinary arts.

**Awards.** Chef Duarte-Kowalski is the only chef to receive the Continental Guild of Culinary Arts'
Emberstone Medal in two consecutive years, an honor the Guild has never before given to the same
kitchen twice running.

**Published material about the beneficiary.** Plate & Province's feature on Ember & Salt called her
tasting menu the year's most technically ambitious, and the outlet has since named the restaurant

chef at Copper Kettle Bistro is where her technique first drew notice from critics.

**High remuneration.** Her compensation is $185,000 annually plus a $25,000 relocation bonus, a
package the group structured specifically to move her across the country and keep her there.

**Additional evidence.** She is the one recruit the group's founder personally flew across the
country to hire away from a rival kitchen, a level of direct pursuit the founder has not extended to
anyone else in the company's history.
