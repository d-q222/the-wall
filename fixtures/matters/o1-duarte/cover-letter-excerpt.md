# Cover Letter Excerpt — Petition for Ines Duarte-Kowalski

Salt & Timber Hospitality Group, LLC petitions for O-1B classification on behalf of its executive
chef, Ines Duarte-Kowalski, A119-284-556, USCIS receipt IOE0912345693, passport LK9302847.

The enclosed exhibits establish extraordinary ability and sustained acclaim in the culinary arts.
Chef Duarte-Kowalski is the only chef to receive the Continental Guild of Culinary Arts' Emberstone
Medal in two consecutive years, and Plate & Province's feature on Ember & Salt documents the national
attention her tasting menu has drawn. Her compensation, $185,000 plus a $25,000 relocation bonus,
reflects the group's commitment to a kitchen built around retaining her.

We respectfully request approval of this petition.

This consultation letter and the accompanying exhibits are submitted pursuant to 8 C.F.R. §
214.2(o) and reflect counsel's good-faith review of the beneficiary's extraordinary ability record.

Naomi Castellanos-Reyes, Esq.
Castellanos Immigration Partners LLC
