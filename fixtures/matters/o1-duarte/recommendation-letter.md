# Recommendation Letter — Ines Duarte-Kowalski

I have covered restaurant openings for twelve years, and I write to support the O-1B petition of
Chef Ines Duarte-Kowalski, executive chef of Ember & Salt under Salt & Timber Hospitality Group, LLC.

I first reviewed her cooking at Copper Kettle Bistro, where she was sous chef, and even then her
plates stood apart from everything else on the block. When Salt & Timber promoted her to executive
chef and opened Ember & Salt around her, she became the youngest executive chef the group has ever
put in charge of a flagship location, a decision that looked risky until the reviews came in.

She is the only chef to receive the Continental Guild of Culinary Arts' Emberstone Medal in two
consecutive years, an honor the Guild has never before given to the same kitchen twice running.
Plate & Province's feature on Ember & Salt called her tasting menu the year's most technically
ambitious, a judgment I share; I have not eaten anything like it elsewhere. She is also one of a
very small number of working chefs asked back to guest-judge the guild's regional finals a second
time.

She is the one recruit the group's founder personally flew across the country to hire away from a
rival kitchen, a level of pursuit I have not seen the founder extend to anyone else in the company's
history. Salt & Timber pays her $185,000 annually plus a $25,000 relocation bonus, compensation that
reflects a kitchen built entirely around retaining her.

Sincerely,
Desmond Okwuosa
