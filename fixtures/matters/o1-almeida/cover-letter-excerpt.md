# Cover Letter Excerpt — Petition for Rafael Almeida-Costa

Canopy Ledger Finance, Inc. petitions for O-1A classification on behalf of its founder and CEO,
Rafael Almeida-Costa, A167-319-285, USCIS receipt IOE0912345739, passport ZM8841205.

The enclosed exhibits establish that Mr. Almeida-Costa has risen to the very top of the
climate-finance sector. He was named to Tidewater Capital's Climate Vanguard Roll three years
running, and Climate Capital Review's profile of the company's $12,750,000 mangrove-restoration
bond documents the acclaim that issuance received across the industry. His compensation,
$230,000, is deliberately modest relative to the value the board believes he has created,
evidence that his recognition rests on achievement rather than salary alone.

We respectfully request approval of this petition.

This consultation letter and the accompanying exhibits are submitted pursuant to 8 C.F.R. §
214.2(o) and reflect counsel's good-faith review of the beneficiary's extraordinary ability record.
