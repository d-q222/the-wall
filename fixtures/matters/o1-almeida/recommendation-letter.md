# Recommendation Letter — Rafael Almeida-Costa

I am a managing partner at Tidewater Capital, and I have worked alongside Rafael Almeida-Costa,
founder and CEO of Canopy Ledger Finance, Inc., since our fund's first investment in the company.
I write to support his petition for O-1A classification in business.

Before founding Canopy Ledger, Rafael structured carbon-credit portfolios at a coastal investment
house, where he is remembered internally for designing the settlement framework his previous
employer still clears through. He left to build something of his own, and the results have been
extraordinary. Canopy Ledger closed the largest mangrove-restoration bond the exchange had listed
that year, a $12,750,000 issuance he led personally through eight months of verification review.

Rafael was named to Tidewater Capital's Climate Vanguard Roll three years running, a distinction
reserved for the small handful of founders our partners believe will define the next decade of
climate finance. Climate Capital Review's profile of the issuance called him the rare founder who
understands both the ecology and the ledger behind a blue-carbon contract, and I have watched
that judgment firsthand in board meetings.

He is also the only founder in our portfolio the exchange's listing committee has personally
invited to ring the opening bell, a level of public recognition we extend to no one else at his
stage. Canopy Ledger compensates him at $230,000 annually, modest relative to the value he has
created. I recommend him without reservation.

Sincerely,
Margaux Delacroix
